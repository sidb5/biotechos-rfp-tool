Hi [First Name],

We've just launched the **BiotechOS Quote Assistant** — a Chrome extension that lets you generate and send preclinical study quotes directly from Gmail.

**Here's what it does:**

- Detects incoming quote requests in your inbox automatically
- Generates a structured quote (scope, timeline, pricing) in seconds using your BiotechOS profile
- Pre-fills your Gmail reply with a professional email — ready to review and send

No switching tabs. No reformatting. No copy-paste.

**→ Install in 2 minutes:**

1. Open Chrome and go to: `chrome://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer Mode** (top-right toggle)
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `gmail-extension-cro` folder
4. Sign in using your existing BiotechOS account

Once installed, open any biotech quote request email in Gmail and look for the **✦ Generate Quote** button in the action bar.
