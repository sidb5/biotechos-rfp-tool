Help & FAQ

Quick Start

1
Sign in to BiotechOS Open the extension popup (click the Bx icon in your Chrome toolbar) and click Sign in to BiotechOS. Log in with your CRO account credentials.
2
Open Gmail and find a quote request Navigate to any email from a biotech or pharma company asking for a preclinical study quote or proposal.
3
Click ✦ Generate Quote A sparkle button appears in the email action bar (next to Reply / Forward). Click it — the quote panel slides in from the right.
4
Review and edit the generated quote The panel shows the parsed request, a suggested scope of work, pricing table, and proposed timeline. Edit any field directly.
5
Click Reply with Quote → Gmail opens a pre-filled reply with your formatted quote and a link to the online quote page. Review the email and click Send.

Frequently Asked Questions

The ✦ Generate Quote button isn't appearing on an email

The button only appears on emails that contain keywords associated with preclinical study requests (e.g. assay, RFP, GLP, quote, in vitro). Internal emails, newsletters, and unrelated messages are intentionally excluded.

If the button is missing on a genuine quote request, try refreshing Gmail (Ctrl+Shift+R) and reopening the email. Make sure you are signed in — the button is hidden when the extension is not authenticated.

I'm signed in on the BiotechOS website but the extension shows "Not signed in"

The extension detects your login via browser cookies. Make sure you are logged in at the same origin configured as the API base (default: http://localhost:3000 for local dev, or your production domain).

Try clicking Re-check connection in the popup. If the problem persists, sign out and sign back in through the popup's Sign in to BiotechOS button.

The quote generation failed with an error

Errors usually fall into two categories:

Auth error — your session expired. Click Sign in in the popup to refresh it.

API error — the BiotechOS backend was unreachable. Check your internet connection and that the API base URL in Advanced settings points to the correct server.

The reply compose window opened but the quote didn't appear in it

This can happen if Gmail's compose overlay didn't fully render within the 5-second timeout. The extension automatically copies the quote HTML to your clipboard as a fallback — paste it into the compose window (Ctrl+Shift+V to paste as rich text in Gmail).

Can I edit the quote after it's been generated?

Yes. Every field in the side panel is editable. Click any text area or input to modify it. The pricing table lets you adjust quantities, unit prices, and descriptions — the total recalculates automatically. Use the Regenerate button at the top of the panel to generate a fresh AI draft if you want to start over.

Where is my quote saved?

Quotes are saved to your BiotechOS account automatically when you click Reply with Quote →. You can view, edit, and share all past quotes from the BiotechOS web app under Requests → Quotes.

How do I point the extension at a different server (staging, production)?

Open the extension popup, expand Advanced settings, and enter the full origin URL (e.g. https://app.biotechos.com). Click Save. The extension will use this as the API base for all requests.

Does the extension read all my emails?

No. The extension reads only the text content of the email thread that is currently open when you click ✦ Generate Quote. It does not scan your inbox, index emails in the background, or send any email content to third parties. All AI processing goes through your BiotechOS account's own API.

Tips & Shortcuts

⌨️ Press Esc to close the quote panel without losing your edits.
🔄 Click Regenerate in the panel header to get a fresh AI draft of the scope while keeping your pricing edits.
🔗 The recipient receives a link to a shareable online quote page — they don't need a BiotechOS account to view it.
🌙 The extension follows your system dark mode preference automatically.
📋 If the compose injection fails, your quote HTML is copied to the clipboard — paste with Ctrl+Shift+V in Gmail for rich-text paste.
